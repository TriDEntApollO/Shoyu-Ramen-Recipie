# Homemade Shoyu Ramen

## Overview

This is a deeply layered homemade shoyu-style ramen built entirely from scratch using rich broth, aromatic oils, chili oil, chewy noodles, soy-cured eggs, pan-seared toppings, and your choice of either soy-glazed chicken or crispy chicken katsu.

This recipe is intentionally detailed and technique-heavy so that even someone cooking ramen for the first time can reproduce the bowl properly.

The recipe balances:

* rich savory broth
* aromatic finishing oils
* sweetness from soy and honey
* chewy noodles
* crispy textures
* spicy chili oil
* deeply umami toppings

The recipe is split into vegetarian and non-vegetarian components wherever possible.

>[!IMPORTANT]
>Read the whole recipe before preparation and ingredients as some parts of the recipe is not linear

# Broth Components

## 1. Non-Vegetarian Chicken Stock

### Optional Roasting Step (Highly Recommended)

For deeper flavor:

Optionally roast:

* chicken meat
* chicken fat

> Do NOT roast the bones.

The roasting can be done:

* in the oven on a tray
  or
* directly in a pan

Roast until lightly browned and aromatic.

> The chicken should not become burnt or deeply charred.
> Only light browning is needed for additional flavor depth in the broth.


### Cleaning the Bones

1. Add the chicken bones into a large pot.

2. Fill with water.

3. Bring rapidly to a boil.

4. Let the bones boil aggressively for a short while (10 to 15 seconds ).

> This removes impurities and blood residue from the bones.

5. Strain the bones and throw away the dirty water.

6. Wash the bones thoroughly under cold running water.

> This step creates a much cleaner tasting broth.


### Mirepoix Preparation

* 2 Large diced (20mm) onion
* 1 sliced carrot
* 4-5 sliced garlic cloves
* 4inch sliced ginger
* 4-5 sliced button mushrooms
* 4-5 sliced Shiitake (or alternatively Oyster) mushrooms
* 1 Large diced apple
* 2 whole green onions, whites sliced and greens roughly chopped each separated

> The vegetables do not need to be finely chopped because the broth will later be strained completely.


### Building the Stock

1. In a large heavy-bottom pot (stock), lightly coat the bottom with neutral oil.
2. Put the flame/heat on high and heat the oil.
3. Drop the onions, carrots, gingers, garlics, whites of green onion
4. Sear, the mix, stir occasionally, each ingredient should be lightly charred 
5. Add your mushrooms and apples and saute for 2-4 minutes
6. Add 1 tablespoon of MSG if not using shiitake mushrooms 
7. Add your cleaned chicken bones, meat and chicken fat
8. Add 3 liters of room temperature water, add extra water if needed to cover/submerge everything in water
9. Rapidly bring the water to a boil on high heat
10. Reduce heat to a medium/medium-low for gentle simmer
11. Add your aromatics, roughly chopped greens of green onion, whole or lightly crushed black pepper 

> Do NOT use finely ground black pepper because it may pass through the strainer later.

12. Optionally add 2 tablespoon of dark soy sauce
13. Partially cover the top with a lid and simmer for 3-4 hours
14. Occasionally skim excess scum or impurities from the surface if necessary.

> Longer simmering creates deeper flavor.


### Straining the Broth

1. Once the broth is done, strain thoroughly through a strainer.

2. Ensure:

   * no vegetable particles
   * no black pepper residue
   * no solids
     remain in the final broth.

3. The final broth should be clean and smooth.


### Optional Step

If you used chicken fat in the stock preparation and after straining the fat is still left whole and did not emulsify into the stock then

1. Separate small amount of the stock, same or more amount than your chicken fat
2. cool it down to room temperature
3. Pour your cooled down stock to a mixer bowl
4. Add your chicken fat
5. turn on the mixer and mix them until properly emulsified
6. Add the emulsified stock back into the original stock and mix well

# 3. The Shoyu Ramen Broth

### Broth Base

1. In a saucepan, lightly coat the bottom with neutral oil.
2. Put it on medium high heat
3. Heat the oil and the pan
4. Add:
   * whites of two sliced green onions
   * two garlic cloves lightly crushed but left whole
   * one inch knob of ginger grated
   * half a finely chopped shallot or red onion
5. Saute for 30-50 seconds until fragrant.

> The aromatics should become fragrant but not browned deeply.

6. Add 4 cups prepared broth
7. Add:
   * 1/4 cup soy sauce
   * 1/2 to 1 tablespoon ground/powdered sugar
   * 1 tablespoon white distilled vinegar

8. Bring to a rapid boil on high heat.
9. Reduce the heat to medium low.
10. Simmer for another 10-15 minutes.
11. Taste and adjust salt and sugar to your liking


### Optional Soy Marinade (Later in the recipe) Reduction Hack

Instead of using regular soy sauce directly:

1. Take the leftover soy marinade used for eggs and paneer/tofu.
2. Strain out:
   * garlic
   * ginger
   * chili flakes
   * solids
3. Simmer the strained marinade until reduced to one-third of its original volume.
4. Add half of this reduction into the broth.

> This creates deeper sweetness because the marinade already contains:
>
> * sugar
> * honey
> * soy sauce
> * sesame oil

5. Add 1/2 a tablespoon of vinegar
6. Simmer the broth for another 10-15 minutes.
7. Taste and adjust salt and sugar to your liking

# 4. Chili Oil

>[!TIP] Reference Video
>[Chili Oil Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9722BdyoWgY)

### Aromatics

Prepare:

* finely diced shallots or red onion
* finely diced garlic
* finely diced ginger

>[!NOTE] Preferred Ratio
> * shallots/onions should be the dominant ingredient
> * garlic should be less than onion
> * ginger should be about half the quantity of garlic
>
> Finely diced ginger is preferred over grated ginger because grated ginger releases too much water.

### The oil

1. Lightly heat or warm 1/4 cup of neutral oil gently in a tempering pan (tadka pan) on medium heat.
2. Add the finely diced shallots or onions and garlic.
4. Cook slowly until fragrant.
5. Add the ginger.
6. Optionally add some thinly sliced fresh chilies.
7. Continue cooking gently until everything has developed a nice brown color.

> Do not bring the heat up to high, keep it in medium
> 
> The aromatics should infuse into the oil without burning.

### The spices

1. While the oil is heating take a heat resistant bowl (ideally metal)
2. Add chili powder (whatever you have Gochugaru, red chili powder, ichimi) .
3. Optionally Sichuan Chili Flakes, or powder
4. Kashimiri chili powder for color
5. Pinch of salt
6. Pinch of MSG
7. Mix everything uniformly

> Adjust spice levels freely according to preference and use any mix of your chili powder combination.

### The Splash

Once the oil is hot and the spice mix is ready

1. Immediately pour the hot oil directly on top of the spice mix
2. Stir everything so the oil infuses with all the spices
3. Add half a tablespoon of dark soy sauce
4. Add a dash of vinegar
5. Let the oil cool down to room temperature

> The more the chili oil rests the deeper the flavour becomes

## 5. Aromatic Oil

### Instructions

1. In a small heavy-bottom pan, combine:
   * 2 tablespoons neutral oil
   * 1/2 tablespoon ghee
or alternatively:
   * 3 tablespoons rendered chicken fat / schmaltz

> If using chicken fat, there is no need for neutral oil or ghee.

2. Heat gently on medium heat until the fat melts completely.
3. Add:
   * chopped green onions
   * lightly crushed whole garlic cloves
3. Fry gently until:
   * the garlic develops light color
   * the oil smells strongly of garlic and green onions
4. Strain the oil.
5. Add:
   * 2 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
   * Optionally white sesame seeds
6. Mix gently.

> This oil is used as the final finishing oil for the ramen.

# 6. Homemade Toasted Sesame Oil Hack

>[!TIP] Reference Video
> [How to Toast Sesame Oil](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4HChSHHd088)

>[!IMPORTANT]
> Do this only if your sesame oil is pale yellow or golden yellow, it is most likely cold-pressed sesame oil and not toasted sesame oil.
> Proper toasted sesame oil should be deep brown.

### Instructions

1. Lightly crush white sesame seeds.

> Do not grind them into powder.

2. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan until lightly browned and fragrant.
3. In a heavy-bottom pan, pour in your untoasted sesame oil.
3. Heat on medium-high briefly.
4. As soon as you can feel heat rising from the oil with your palm, immediately reduce the heat to low or medium-low.

> Do not overheat the oil.
>
> Extremely high heat ruins the flavor.

8. Add the toasted sesame seeds into the oil.
9. Let the oil infuse for 10-15 minutes.
10. Continue heating until the oil develops a deep brown color.
11. Once the oil becomes dark brown turn off the heat
12. Let it cool down to room temperature
13. Strain the oil or optionally leave the sesame seeds in the oil.

>[!NOTE] Sesame Seed to Oil Ratio
> Ideal:
> * 40% sesame seeds
> * 60% sesame oil
>
> Minimum recommended:
> * 30% sesame seeds
> * 70% sesame oil

# Vegetarian Toppings

## 7. Soy-Marinated Paneer / Tofu

### Soy Marinade 

1. Lightly crush:
   * 2 medium sized garlic cloves
   * 2 medium sized ginger slices

> Crush enough to break fibers and release flavor while still keeping them whole.

2. Into a bowl add:
   * 1/2 cup dark soy sauce
   * 1/4 cup sugar
   * 1 tablespoon honey
   * 1 tablespoon vinegar
   * The garlic cloves
   * The ginger slices
   * optional sliced green or red chilies
   * 1 tablespoon chili flakes
   * 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil
   * 1/2 cup water
3. Mix everything thoroughly.

### Marinating the Paneer/Tofu

1. Cut paneer or tofu into long rectangular cuboids.
3. Place paneer/tofu pieces into the marinade.
4. Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap.
5. Push the plastic wrap directly onto the paneer/tofu pieces so the pieces remain fully submerged.
6. Optionally place a small weight on top.

> Any exposed surface may leave white unmarinated patches.

7. Marinate for 8 hours or more.

>[!TIP]
>  If using the same marinade:
>  1. Marinate the eggs first.
>  2. After 12-14 hours, add the paneer or tofu.
>  3. Continue marinating everything together for another 8-10 hours.

### Pan Searing

1. Remove the paneer or tofu from the marinade.
2. Let excess marinade drip off slightly.
3. Lightly coat a pan with neutral oil.
4. Heat the pan on high briefly.
5. Reduce the heat to medium.
6. Drop in your marinated paneer/tofu.
7. Sear all sides until:
   * browned
   * lightly crusted
   * slightly caramelized from the soy marinade

## 6. Brown Butter Sweet Corn

### Instructions

1. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a pan over medium heat.
2. Let the butter brown slowly.
3. Swirl the pan occasionally so the butter browns evenly without burning.
4. Once the butter develops a nutty brown aroma and color, add 3 tablespoons sweet corn
4. Toss and baste the corn repeatedly using the browned butter.
5. Continue cooking until lightly caramelized.
6. Optionally season with:
   * black pepper
   * salt

> If using salted butter, additional salt is not necessary.

# Non-Vegetarian Toppings

## 8. Soy-Cured Eggs (Ajitama)

### Soft-Boiled Eggs

1. Fill a pot with water.
2. Bring it to a boil.
3. Add:
   * 2 tablespoons vinegar
   * optionally 1-2 pinches salt

> Vinegar helps loosen the shell and makes the eggs easier to peel.

4. Carefully lower the eggs into the boiling water.
5. For cold eggs boil for 7 minutes
6. For room temp eggs boil for 6-7 minutes

>[!NOTE]
> ***6 minutes:*** runnier yolk
> 
> ***7 minutes:*** jammy yolk

5. After boiling immediately transfer the eggs into an cold ice bath.
6. Leave in the ice bath for about 5 minutes.

> This immediately stops the cooking process.

>[!TIP] Peeling Hack
> 1. Tap the shell gently all around using the back of a spoon.
> 2. Crack the shell thoroughly.
> 3. Slide the spoon underneath the shell.
> 4. Carefully peel away the shell.

### Soy Marinade 

1. Lightly crush:
   * 2 medium sized garlic cloves
   * 2 medium sized ginger slices

> Crush enough to break fibers and release flavor while still keeping them whole.

2. Into a bowl add:
   * 1/2 cup dark soy sauce
   * 1/4 cup sugar
   * 1 tablespoon honey
   * 1 tablespoon vinegar
   * The garlic cloves
   * The ginger slices
   * optional sliced green or red chilies
   * 1 tablespoon chili flakes
   * 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil
   * 1/2 cup water
3. Mix everything thoroughly.

### Marinating the Eggs

1. Place peeled eggs into the marinade.
2. Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap.
3. Push the plastic wrap directly onto the eggs so the eggs remain fully submerged.
4. Optionally place a small weight on top.

> Any exposed surface may leave white unmarinated patches.

5. Marinate for 20-24 hours or more

---

> But taste wise and enjoyment wise I much prefer you to make the Chicken Katsu.
> Both will serve as a good ramen topping, but if you are willing to go that extra mile, I highly lean on the Chicken Katsu.

## 8. Soy-Glazed Chicken

>[!TIP] Reference Video
> [If you only have 20 minutes, make Soy Glazed Chicken!](https://youtube.com/shorts/nL8HDnxKLvg?si=lpyGr4qpn8aGQj8m)

### Chicken Preparation

1. Use boneless skin-on chicken thighs.
2. Place the chicken onto a cutting board or flat surface.
3. Cover with plastic wrap.
4. Beat the meat with a meat mallet or heavy pan or pot
5. Flatten until approximately half-inch thick.

> Flattening helps the chicken cook evenly.

### Marination

1. Season both sides with:
   * salt
   * black pepper
   * small dash of sesame oil

2. Marinate for ideally 2-4 hours, or a minimum of 30 minutes

> Longer marination improves flavor.

### Cooking the Chicken

1. Coat both sides lightly with corn flour.
2. Shake off excess corn flour.
3. Add a thin layer of neutral oil into a pan.
4. Heat on high until the pan becomes hot.
5. Reduce the heat to medium.
6. Place the chicken skin-side down first.
7. Cook for 7 minutes.
8. Flip the chicken.
9. Cook the second side for another 5-6 minutes.
10. Remove the chicken from the pan.

### Soy Glaze

* Soy sauce
* Dark soy sauce
* Honey
* Garlic
* Water

>[!NOTE] Garlic Options
> ***For only garlicky flavour :*** Lightly crushed garlic cloves
>
> ***For garlic bits on the chicken :*** Minced or finely chopped garlic

### Glazing Instructions

1. In the same pan used for the chicken, pour in the glaze mixture.

> The leftover oil and fond improve flavor.

2. Simmer for around 1 minute until slightly thickened.
3. Allow the water to reduce.
4. Once bubbling for 10-20 seconds, Add back the chicken into the pan.
5. Spoon glaze over the top repeatedly and flipping the chicken occasionally.
6. Cook the chicken for 1-2 minutes on high heat until fully coated.
7. Slice into thick strips before serving.

## 9. Chicken Katsu

>[!TIP] Reference Video
> [The Easiest Homemade Chicken Katsu](https://youtu.be/D6diqH8RF4U?si=FKJgOQg_pZr63S5N)

### Chicken Preparation

1. Use boneless skin-less chicken breasts.
2. Place the chicken onto a cutting board or flat surface.
3. Cover with plastic wrap.
4. Beat the meat with a meat mallet or heavy pan or pot
5. Flatten until approximately half-inch thick.

> Flattening helps the chicken cook evenly.


# Marination

1. Season both sides with:
   * salt
   * black pepper
   * small dash of sesame oil

2. Marinate for ideally 2-4 hours, or a minimum of 30 minutes

> Longer marination improves flavor.

### Breading Station

Prepare three trays for:

1. All-purpose flour
2. Egg wash
3. Panko breadcrumbs

### Egg Wash

Crack eggs into a tray or bowl and whisk until:

* yolks
* whites
  are completely combined.

### Optional Seasoning

Season the flour and egg wash with:

* salt
* black pepper
* chili powder

>[!TIP] Tip for Breading
> The key trick is using:
> * one dry hand
> * one wet hand
> 
>This prevents clumping and keeps the breading clean.

### 1. Flour Layer

1. Place the chicken into the flour.
2. Press firmly using your dry hand.
3. Flip and repeat.
4. Ensure the entire surface is coated.

### 2. Egg Wash Layer

1. Transfer the chicken into the egg wash.
2. Use:
   * dry hand for dry surfaces
   * wet hand for wet surfaces

3. Press gently so the egg binds fully to the flour coating.

### 3. Breadcrumb Layer

1. Transfer the chicken into the breadcrumbs using the wet hand.
2. Press firmly so breadcrumbs stick properly.
3. Flip the chicken.
4. Use your dry hand once the breadcrumb side becomes dry.
5. Continue pressing firmly until:
   * no exposed meat
   * no exposed flour
   * no visible egg wash remains
6. Only breadcrumbs should remain visible.

### Oil Prep

1. Use a heavy-bottom pan.
2. Ideally use a pan close to the size of the chicken.

> This helps reduce oil usage.

3. Ideally the oil should fully submerge the chicken.
4. At minimum, the oil should cover half the chicken.
5. Heat the oil on medium/medium-high to an ideal frying temperature of 350Â°F (175Â°C).

>[!TIP] Oil Temperature Test
>
> 1. Heat the oil on high.
> 2. Dip a wooden utensil into the oil.
> 3. If bubbles rapidly form around the wood, the oil is ready.
> 4. Reduce the heat to medium before frying.

> High heat should only be used to preheat the oil faster.

### Frying

1. Lay down the chicken in the pan facing away from you.
2. Fry until:
   * deep brown
   * fully crispy

3. Ensure all breadcrumbs become evenly browned.
4. Remove and drain excess oil.
5. Slice into thick strips before serving.

## 10. Homemade Panko Breadcrumb Hack

>[!TIP] Reference Video
> [Never buy panko again!!](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/rHcBHM8XDRw)

### Instructions

1. Take plain white bread.
2. Remove all four crust edges.
3. Use only the soft white center.
4. Cut into medium chunks.
5. Place into food processor or manual vegetable chopper.

> Do NOT use a mixer grinder.

6. Pulse until medium flaky crumbs form.

>[!NOTE]
> The crumbs should not become powder.
>
> The crumbs should remain medium and flaky.

7. Heat a pan gently on low heat.
8. Add the breadcrumbs.
9. Toast on low heat for 10-15 minutes.
10. Stir periodically so they don't brown or burn.
11. Allow the breadcrumbs to dry out slowly.

> The breadcrumbs should:
>
> * become crispy
> * lose moisture
> * remain white
>
> They should not brown.

# 11. Noodles

For noodles you have 2 options

>[!WARNING]
> Do not use instant ramen noodles or maggi noodles. They have wavy texture, absorbs broth too quick and becomes soggy and doesn't provide the chewy texture along with the satisfying slurp.

### 1. Ramen Noodles

Ramen noodles are made with wheat and alkaline water which gives it that yellowish tint and springiness.
Do not use deep fried ramen noodles.

1. Cook according to package instructions.
2. Strain the noodles
3. Transfer into a cold ice bath if not being directly used.
4. Leave in the ice bath for 3-5 minutes.
5. Strain thoroughly.

> Ramen noodles should remain slightly chewy.


### 2. Hakka Noodles Alternative

1. Bring water to a boil.
2. Add the noodles.
3. Cook for 2-3 minutes maximum.
4. Check texture frequently.
5. Once slightly chewy immediately transfer into an ice bath
6. Leave in the ice bath for 3-5 minutes.
7. Strain thoroughly.

> Ice water stops the cooking process and preserves chewiness.

# 12. Final Bowl Assembly

### Bowl Choice

Use a large tapered ramen bowl:
   * narrower at the bottom
   * wider at the top

> This shape helps properly layer broth, noodles, toppings, and oils.

### Assembly Order

1. Pour hot broth into the bowl.
2. Add noodles.
3. Add chili oil according to your spice preference.
4. Optionally Lightly mix broth, noodles and chili oil, so the chili oil infuses evenly throughout the bowl.

### Toppings

Arrange:
1. Ajitama
2. Brown butter sweet corn
3. Sauteed mushrooms
4. Chicken katsu or soy-glazed chicken
5. Soy-marinated paneer or tofu

Or any toppings of your choice

### Final Garnishes

1. Add thinly sliced green onions in the center.
2. Drizzle aromatic oil over everything.
3. Sprinkle sesame seeds if not already present in the aromatic oil.


And there you go your ramen bowl is ready to be devoured.
